Work

Things that I've worked on inside and outside of class as an Olin College student!

For my final project in Computer Graphics (Wellesley College CS307), I made a koi pond with three.js!
I started a club on campus called "Computing Conversations", which meets once per week to talk about a special topic to help spark appreciation and interest among people of all majors and skill levels!
Project Eye-Helper: an ongoing research pursuit in the Olin College Crowdsourcing and Machine Learning Lab - our goal is create a device that utilizes a combination of computer vision and crowdsourcing to help blind people shop for groceries without needing to rely on sighted in-store personnel.
For my final project in Computational Robotics, Franz and I programmed a robot to try searching for samples in unknown environments (like Mars).
In Discrete Math, Jacob and I gave a presentation on the group theory behind making pretty patterns on Rubik's cubes!
I teamed up with Ryan and Mike to develop and execute curriculum for the "Do Machine Learning" student group at Olin College!
